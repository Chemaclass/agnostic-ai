### General

- The companion lists Claude Code agent team teammates as subagents while they work, and shows subagents as a tree: each under the one that started it, with each level folding (#3905).
- A click on a Claude Code teammate in a tmux pane brings that pane forward. Any other subagent brings its chat forward and shows how to reach the subagent at once, without taking the keys from the terminal. A subagent that just finished still opens its chat (#3905).

### General

- The companion installed from the terminal now has the update entry and the ⋯ badge too. On macOS, **Update to vX.Y.Z** runs `agnostic-ai upgrade` and restarts the companion; when that cannot run from the app, the list shows the command with **Copy command**. New `agnostic-ai companion update-status --json` reports the installed and latest stable release and how to upgrade (#3850).

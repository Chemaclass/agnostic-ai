---
name: plain-english
description: Write docs and comments in plain English. Avoid em dashes and verbosity.
globs: "**/*.md"
alwaysApply: true
---

- No em dashes (`—`). Use period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
- No filler words: "just", "really", "basically", "actually", "simply".
- Short synonyms over long phrases. "Fix" not "implement a solution for".
- One idea per sentence. Break long sentences in two.
- Lead with the action or fact. Background after.
- Avoid hedging ("might", "perhaps", "consider"). State the recommendation.

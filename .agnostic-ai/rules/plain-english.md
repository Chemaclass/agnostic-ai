---
name: plain-english
description: Write docs and comments in plain English. Avoid em dashes and verbosity.
globs: "**/*.md"
alwaysApply: true
---

- No em or en dashes. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
- No filler ("just", "really", "basically") or hedging ("might", "consider"). State the recommendation.
- Short words, one idea per sentence, action or fact first.
- One point per bullet. Name the effect a reader sees; leave the mechanism and every case to the code, PR, or reference page.
- No throat-clearing, closing summary, or restated guarantee ("writes nothing").
